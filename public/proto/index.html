PROTOTYPES

Test builds

Rough playable sketches. Not part of the arcade.
Play each, then tell me which feel fun.

Nova
Tap anywhere to detonate. Chain the blast through as many orbs as you can.
Pin
Tap to fire pins into a spinning core. Clip another pin and it is over.
Span
Hold to grow a beam, release to drop it across the gap. Too short or too long and you fall.
Prism
Tap to cycle the ring's color. Match every falling shard as it lands.
Apex
Hold to throttle, release to brake. Hit every apex right at the grip limit while a wall of light chases you.

Winners get the full treatment: phases, leaderboards, hub cards.
Losers get deleted.
